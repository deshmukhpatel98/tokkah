TAPE — fatigue lab

A/B test for the six known causes of Zoom fatigue. Ordinary WebRTC transport on purpose — these fixes are independent of the pipeline, so they can be tested before it exists. Hold SPACE during a call to flip every fix off and feel the difference.

1 · Screen scale

Hold a credit card (or any ID card) against the screen and drag the right edge until the blue box matches its width exactly. This is the only way a browser can learn your display's real physical size — without it, "life size" is a guess.

2 · How far are you sitting?

Eye-to-screen distance. Typical laptop use is 50–70 cm.

3 · Your face

Drag the green line to your eyes, and the yellow edges to the sides of your head (temple to temple). Your peer needs this to render you at true physical scale.

(adult average ≈ 150 mm)

4 · Your breath — test this one alone

The single most valuable 200 ms in the design. Before you speak, you inhale — and that inhale reaches the other person before your words do. Every call app deletes it as noise; we send it first. Say a few sentences with pauses between them, and watch what the lead actually measures on your voice.
