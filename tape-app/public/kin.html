Download for Mac

v0.147.0 · 1.5 MB · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon · free

Open the .dmg, double-click Kin. macOS warns once, because we sign it ourselves and Apple has not notarised it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway (or right-click the app → Open). That is the only step — after it, the app moves itself into /Applications and keeps itself up to date. Nothing else to do.

Prefer no security prompt at all, and comfortable with a terminal? curl -fsSL https://room.tokkah.com/macos/install.sh | sh — a download by curl is never quarantined, so it never meets Gatekeeper.
