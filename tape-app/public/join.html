Get Kin for Mac

v0.97.0 · 1.5 MB · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon · free

Open the .dmg, double-click Kin. macOS warns once, because we sign it ourselves and Apple has not notarised it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway (or right-click the app → Open). That is the only step — after it the app moves itself into /Applications and keeps itself up to date.

Prefer no security prompt at all, and comfortable with a terminal? A download by curl is never quarantined, so it never meets Gatekeeper. curl -fsSL https://room.tokkah.com/macos/install.sh | sh

Already have Kin? The link is the part that carries the room, so press Try the link again below, or paste it into a browser on the Mac that has Kin. Opening Kin on its own starts a new call rather than joining this one.

Kin is Mac-only today. There is no browser call and no Windows, Linux, iPhone or Android build yet. To join this call you need a Mac (macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon) with Kin on it — the room name above is all you have to carry over to it.

A link does not ring anybody, so both people have to be in this room at the same moment; once you know someone's name in Kin you can ring them directly instead, and their Mac rings whether or not the app is open. Nothing is stored either way. The room name is also the encryption key, so for anything you would not say in public use a fresh one; it never leaves the two machines.
