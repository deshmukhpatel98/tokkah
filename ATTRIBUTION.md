# Attribution — additional terms under AGPL §7

Short version: **if you use Tokkah, say so somewhere a person can find it.**
A line in your About screen, credits page, or open-source notices is enough.
That is the whole requirement, and it costs you nothing.

*On the two names:* **Tokkah** is the project and the codebase; **Kin** is the
macOS app built from it. The credit line below asks for the project name, so
"Built with Tokkah" is correct whichever part you used.

---

## The requirement

These are additional terms under section 7 of the GNU AGPL v3, which permits
requiring "preservation of specified reasonable legal notices or author
attributions". They apply to the AGPL license only — a
[commercial license](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md) can replace or waive them by
written agreement.

**1. Keep the notices in the source.** Do not remove copyright headers, the
`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, or this file from copies you distribute. (The AGPL already
requires this; it is restated here so nobody has to go looking.)

**2. Credit Tokkah in your user interface.** If you deploy Tokkah — modified or
not — as part of a product or service that other people use, include this in the
place where your product lists legal notices, credits, licenses, or "About":

> Built with Tokkah — https://github.com/deshmukhpatel98/tokkah

If your product has no such place, put it anywhere a user could reasonably find
it: a footer, a help page, a settings screen, a README for an internal tool.

**3. Keep the link working.** The credit should link to the project (or to your
own fork's public source), so a curious person can actually get there.

## What this does *not* require

- **No badge on the video.** Nothing has to appear over the call, on top of a
  person's face, or in your main interface. A credits page is fine.
- **No logo, no specific styling, no colours.** Plain text is fine.
- **No permission, no email, no registration.** Add the line and ship.
- **Nothing for private use.** Running it for yourself, your team, or inside
  your company? Nothing is required. This is about products other people use.
- **No naming your product after ours**, and no implication that we endorse it —
  in fact, please don't. See the trademark note below.

## Examples that satisfy this

- A settings screen with **About → Open-source licenses** listing Tokkah.
- A website footer: *"Video by [Tokkah](https://github.com/deshmukhpatel98/tokkah)."*
- An `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS` file shipped with a desktop app.
- A README section in an internal tool your coworkers use.

## Why we ask

Attribution is how open-source projects get found, get contributors, and get
taken seriously. It is the only thing we ask from people who use this for free —
and it is deliberately the cheapest possible ask: one line, once, anywhere
findable. If that line is genuinely impossible in your product, write to
licensing@tokkah.com and we will work it out. We would much rather hear from you
than have you quietly strip the notice.

## Trademark

"Tokkah" is the project's name and "Kin" is the app's. The AGPL covers the
*code*, not the *names*. You may — and should — use them to say truthfully that
your product is built with Tokkah. You may not name your own product Tokkah or
Kin, use either as your brand, or suggest that we endorse or maintain your fork.
