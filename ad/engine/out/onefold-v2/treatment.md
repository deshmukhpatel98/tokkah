TREATMENT

**Tension:** You keep running, but the worn-out shoes keep staying.

**Idea:** Show one shoe becoming the next, through the material already inside it.

**Appearance:** Onefold has a low-profile road-running silhouette; a continuous knit upper and sole; one warm sand-amber material; a soft, rounded heel; and a subtle heel-to-toe fold line, without a separate logo. One canonical layered SVG, `lib.product`, supplies every shoe, including pairs and worn versions; wear is an overlay, never a redesign.

**Motif:** Light travels along the shoe’s own fold line, activating the worn-to-new transformation. It returns at S1’s opening transformation, S5’s wear threshold, S9’s reconstructed pair and S13’s final invitation. This is graphic recycling, not a suggestion that the shoe folds or repairs itself.

The 1920×1080 world is near-black, with cream typography, warm amber product light and cold blue drawer geometry. No footage, floating symbols or decorative loops. Cuts follow the score; secondary reveals take 320ms. We discover the circular idea through the actual shoe, its knit and its return—not through a sustainability diagram.
