# Kin film v2: direction record (2026-09-05)

Two director's treatments were produced by GPT-6 Astra (via the Experiential
Labs gateway, reasoning_effort=high; max exceeded the gateway's 10-minute
deadline twice). They are kept verbatim beside this file. The orchestrator
(Claude) reconciled them as follows.

## Kept from the v1 brief
- 75.0 s, 1920x1080, shot boundaries S1..S15 unchanged (so render.mjs, the
  hero cut 26-38 s and the site embed keep working).
- Palette, fonts, three canvases, deterministic render(t), public kinAd API,
  query flags, CSP-separate JS, mobile half-res canvases, sound pill.
- Non-negotiables: no red, plain words, nothing over a face, edge never dark
  during a call, cream = you, blue = them.

## Adopted from treatment A (Acts I-II, poster)
- Governing idea: the film begins by losing someone; Kin brings them back.
- Act I: two portrait fields instead of orbs; a three-stroke light phrase
  (180/280/140 ms strokes) instead of ripples; a failed hand-off (two starts,
  two retreats) instead of a particle shatter; S3 degrades the REAL portrait
  (source-sampled blocks, growing 8%->22%->38%), S4 repairs it in place.
- Act II: the repaired stroke bends into the window frame; room is three
  architectural light fields, no bokeh discs; portrait is a 12-plane study
  with differential softness; edge band clipped below the title strip and
  computed from distance-to-edge; S9 splits into two large windows with a
  72 px gap; S10 never shrinks the people.
- Poster: the person in the window at full exposure, lockup in the right
  column, play control over empty room. No dimming.

## Adopted from treatment B (Acts III-IV, type, score)
- S11: opaque lit planet, authored coastlines, right half of frame; type in
  the left column; "40 ms" fixed in the column, shown only after the return.
- S12: forty routes in four groups, drawn by their heads, occluded by the
  planet, settling to 22%.
- S13: two large call windows return (product proof) instead of dots.
- S14/S15: mark with body before glow; "Kin" 204 px -> 152 px; one end card.
- Type system: 88/72/64/56 px; entrances 480 ms, exits 280 ms.
- Score: three-event motif (0/310/790 ms), harmony A-E -> D-A -> D-A-E ->
  F# at 64.0; rendered once to a buffer; ~-21 LUFS, < -3 dBTP.
