Six numbers we can defend

If it is not measured, it is not on this page.

  1. 0.0% concealed speech on a clean path 48 kHz / 24-bit PCM, bit-exact end to end. Concealment 0.0% on the clean path. Under 5% loss: 2.1%. Before the framing and stripe fixes: 36–41%.
  2. 8ms to repair a lost audio packet Sliding-window FEC, p50 8 ms / p95 56 ms. A classic block code, RS(10,13), needs the whole 80 ms group. 8 of 8 paired calls at 5% loss: 5× less concealment and −44 ms end-to-end.
  3. 23.0ms glass-to-glass, p50 n=10, ±1.7 ms. Timecoded fixture. Receiver display minus sender preview. Both browsers on one machine — this is the pipeline floor, not a real-world route. Live production decomposition: ~36–45 ms.
  4. 99.7 VMAF at the design point Fixed QP24, 30 fps, Chromium, cam1080 fixture. The rig’s own transparency floor. Safari cannot run quantizer mode and uses VBR; that arm is scored separately and is not entitled to this number.
  5. 347ms second person, click → connected Live A/B, 5 calls per arm. Lobby pre-dials in hold state. Was 565 ms (−39%). Host or guest: one click. Zero is illegal — getUserMedia needs a gesture.
  6. $0 to run, below ~5,000 calls a month One Worker + one Durable Object. Media is peer-to-peer. Direct call ≈ $0.0001/min. Relayed minute $0.0075. Inside Cloudflare’s free tiers the bill is zero. Not “100× cheaper” — that claim was withdrawn.

The inversion

Quality is a constant. Time is the only shock absorber.

Every calling stack built since 2011 spends picture quality when the network dips. The voice goes thin. The codec invents syllables you never said. The face goes mushy. That is not a bug. It is the architecture.

Tokkah does not lower quality. When the link cannot carry a frame, the frame is dropped before encode, so the reference chain has no holes. When a packet dies, a sliding window rebuilds it in about eight milliseconds. When even that is not enough, the call holds a pristine picture and says so. It never spends the face.

What exists, today, in production

Not a deck. A shipping call.

The voice is the recording.

No Opus. No “HD voice.” Mic → 8 ms PCM frames → lossless Rice coding (~0.56× on speech) → six striped datachannels → sliding-window repair → playout. On a clean path the far end plays the bits you captured.

Lane rate ~0.73–0.94 Mbps after compression and group-sized parity. Encode 25 µs/frame.

The picture is the camera’s.

720p in is 720p out. 1080p in is 1080p out. We used to upscale everything to 1080. That spent 19% of the stream and 3.8 ms p50 for zero extra picture. The encoder now follows the sensor.

Under RTT 80 ms / 1% loss: held 1080p, 0 frames lost. Stock WebRTC on the same path collapsed to 720p, 460–468 packets lost, 2 freezes.

The whole backend is one Worker.

Signalling and (optionally) short-lived TURN credentials. Media never touches a server. Embed is one script tag. A stranger can clone, npm ci, and deploy on a free Cloudflare account. Bundle 52.6 KiB gzipped.

Zero runtime dependencies in the shipped app. AGPL v3, plus a commercial license for closed products.

Self-view is off.

You hold a button to peek. Chronic self-view is the strongest published correlate of video-call fatigue. The audio buffer used to ratchet after a bad patch and stay fat for minutes — the other person just seemed slow. It now measures arrival spread and gives the latency back.

We do not claim a validated fatigue-scale score. No published study links ZEF to measured delay. This is the delay-literature proxy, stated as such.

Echo, in a real room.

In-house canceller on the raw PCM lane. Lab: 62 dB ERLE, double-talk survives at 0.95 correlation, bit-exact passthrough when the far end is silent. First real calls (two Macs, one room, speakers): detector fired, do-no-harm gate opened, 3.6 dB delivered. Filter learns always; it only plays when ERLE ≥ 3 dB.

A canceller that cannot prove it is helping is not allowed to touch the lossless lane.

You talk. They hear their language.

One globe tap, or data-translate="es" on the embed. Gemini live translation is the default on production. Probe: end-of-phrase → translated audio 164 ms. The lossless lane is never touched — if the interpreter falls behind it degrades to captions, never to stalled sound.

Per-room daily budget, server-enforced. Over budget the socket is accepted, told, and closed.

Surfaces, not slides

  • Real Safari ↔ Chromium on production: custom video lane, no fallback, 0.091% concealment, lossless audio 1.09 Mbps, picture both ways.
  • Mobile Safari (iOS simulator): SharedArrayBuffer path works. After the do-no-harm AEC gate, 0.067% concealment on a 2-minute soak.
  • 10-minute live soak on the latency-floor default: depth held 13–21 ms the whole way. Mouth-to-ear 43.7–51.5 ms. Concealment 0.008%. No ratchet.
  • First human calls on the new stack, 2026-08-11: echo cancelled in air, burst shield and video-duress engaged and recovered, camera flips held, fleet mouth-to-ear p50 48.3 ms.

What we will not say

An investor who later reads the lab notebook should find nothing to unsay.

  • We have not measured Zoom or Google Meet. Their join is account-gated or bot-gated. The instrument exists and is validated against our own app (23.0 vs an independent 27.3 ms). We will not invent their number. A community Jitsi in Munich measured 179 ms — almost all of that is the round trip to Munich, not their pipeline. Against a well-sited SFU the routing advantage is single-digit milliseconds. Any real lead has to come from the pipeline, which we have not compared.
  • Anywhere-to-anywhere under 150 ms is the standing goal, not a trophy. Device pipeline, loopback: 48 ms mouth-to-ear, 45 ms glass-to-glass. First mile from Delhi, real STUN: 5.37 ms RTT. India ↔ US is still roughly 25–40 ms short on the paths we have measured. A real cross-planet media call has been instrumented, not yet taken. We will not model that last hop into a headline.
  • Three-person rooms are built and verified. They are off in production. Full mesh on staging: 9/9 assertions, 10.6–11.7 Mbps uplink per device. A third joiner on room.tokkah.com is refused. The flip is one line, and it stays a product decision — phones have not been thermally proven, and translation turns off at three.
  • We do not claim people are less tired. Self-view is off because that is Bailenson’s published remedy. The buffer no longer stays fat after the network heals — measured. Video delay stays flat under 5% loss — measured. The ZEF scale has never been correlated with latency in the literature. We will not pretend it has.
  • Several numbers on this project were published and then withdrawn. “2.4× better than same-city Zoom.” “100× cheaper.” “1920×1080 delivered” from a 720p camera. “6.6× lower latency than an SFU.” Each one is still in MEASURED.md, marked untrue, with the method that killed it. That file is the product’s character.
